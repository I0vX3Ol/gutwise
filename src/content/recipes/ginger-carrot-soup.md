Some days the gut is simply unhappy and the idea of cooking anything with a technique to it is unbearable. This soup exists for those days. It is chopping, simmering and blending — no browning to judge, no timing to get right, nothing that punishes inattention.

## The stock problem

If you take one thing from this page, take this: commercial stock is where the elimination phase quietly fails. Onion and garlic are in nearly every cube, powder and carton on the shelf, usually described as "natural flavouring" or "vegetable extract" rather than named outright. People do everything else correctly, season with a stock cube, and cannot understand why their symptoms have not settled.

You have two good options. Buy a stock certified by Monash or FODMAP Friendly, or use plain water and lean harder on the ginger, turmeric, salt and lemon. Water genuinely works here — the carrots carry enough sweetness on their own that the soup does not taste thin.

## Why carrots are worth leaning on

Carrots are among a small handful of vegetables with no FODMAP ceiling whatsoever. During elimination, when almost every decision involves weighing a portion, having a few ingredients you can use freely lowers the daily cognitive load considerably. Potato, spinach, cucumber, courgette in moderation, and carrot are the ones worth memorising.
