Search for a low-FODMAP shopping list and you will be handed forty products, most of them expensive specialty items, many of them things you do not need at all. It is a category with an unusually high ratio of marketing to substance, and newly diagnosed readers are a soft target.

This is the short list. Seven things, roughly in the order we would buy them, and honest notes on where the money is genuinely wasted.

## The principle worth holding on to

Almost everything you eat during elimination is ordinary food. Chicken, eggs, rice, potatoes, carrots, spinach, oats, strawberries, lactose-free milk — none of it is specialty, none of it carries a premium, and all of it is in your normal supermarket.

Specialty products are worth paying for in exactly one situation: where an ordinary product hides high-FODMAP ingredients behind vague labelling, and you cannot tell by reading. That describes stock, sauces, bread, protein powders and snack bars. It does not describe most of the aisle.

If you buy two things from this page, buy the Monash app and one certified stock. Those two purchases prevent more failed elimination phases than the other five combined.
